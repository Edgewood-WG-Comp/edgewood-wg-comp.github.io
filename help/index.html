Getting started

For families and students · 5 short videos, about 5:25 in all

New to SOS Crew? Watch these in order: sign in, find a shift, your shifts, what you owe, and coordinator spots.

Start with Part 1

1. Sign in or set up your family
1:25 · Sign in with Google or your email, or set up your family if you are new. Students can sign themselves up too.
2. Find a shift and sign up
1:16 · Pick a day, open a block (Early, Late…), and sign up the person who will work it. The filter narrows the list.
3. Your shifts, point person and reminders
1:07 · See every shift your family has, reach your point person on contest weekend, cancel if you need to, and put it all on your phone calendar.
4. What your family owes
0:52 · Your family owes shifts for each student plus parent shifts. See where you stand, check background checks, and add anyone missing.
5. Put your name in for coordinator spots
0:46 · Some spots are filled by the coordinators. Put your name in for one; it does not count until you are placed.

Contest day

For volunteers, point people and crews
0. How contest-day tracking works: one guard, start to finish
1:56 · Follow Edgewood through the day: check-in, home room, warm-up and inspection, props, the floor, and loading out, and see each station's tap show up everywhere else.
1. Guides: your guard, all day
1:38 · Guides stay with one guard from check-in until its home room is cleared, tapping each step as it happens.
2. Check-in desk: guards, directors and home rooms
1:07 · Check in each guard and its director, give the guard a home room, and keep track of which rooms are free.
3. Prop crews: unload, prop area and loading
1:00 · Check props in at the door, hold and stage them for warm-up, and mark them loaded when they leave.
4. Inspection and the performance door
0:26 · Inspectors mark equipment complete; the performance door logs on-floor and off-floor times.
5. Point people: your station page
0:56 · Your page lists everyone on your station with call and text buttons, group texts, and check-in. Guide coordinators assign guards.
6. The HQ board and the public board
1:16 · HQ sees every guard: where it is, its schedule, home room, inspection, director, and anything running late. The public board shows just the schools.
