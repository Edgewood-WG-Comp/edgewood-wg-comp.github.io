Getting started

For families and students · 5 short videos, about 5:13 in all

New to SOS Crew? Watch these in order: sign in, find a shift, your shifts, what you owe, and coordinator spots.

Start with Part 1

1. Sign in or set up your family
1:25 · Sign in with Google or your email, or set up your family if you are new. Students can sign themselves up too.
2. Find a shift and sign up
1:10 · Pick a day, open a time slot, and sign up the person who will work it. The filter narrows the list.
3. Your shifts, point person and reminders
1:07 · See every shift your family has, reach your point person on contest weekend, cancel if you need to, and put it all on your phone calendar.
4. What your family owes
0:46 · Your family owes shifts for each student plus parent shifts. See where you stand, check background checks, and add anyone missing.
5. Put your name in for coordinator spots
0:46 · Some spots are filled by the coordinators. Put your name in for one; it does not count until you are placed.

Contest day

For volunteers, point people and crews
1. Homeroom guides: tracking your guard
1:04 · Homeroom guides tap where their guard is during their shift, and only for the guard they are assigned to.
2. Point people: your station page
0:55 · Your page lists everyone on your station with call and text buttons, group texts by time slot, and check-in.
3. Tracker crews: tapping steps
0:34 · Crews at the prop door, check-in, warm-up and the gym tap each guard's steps as they happen.
4. Reading the live board
0:55 · The live board shows every guard: where the guard and props are, who is performing, and what is running late.
